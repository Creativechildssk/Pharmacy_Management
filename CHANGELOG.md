# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026

- Estate employee and external-patient pharmacy workflows
- Visiting-doctor prescription capture by pharmacy staff
- Batch-wise FEFO inventory and dispensing
- Stock receipts with supplier, invoice, batch and expiry details
- Expired-batch blocking and near-expiry visibility
- Controlled damage, expiry, supplier-return and stock adjustments
- Dispensing and stock movement ledger with historical cost snapshots
- Operational and management cost/value reports with CSV export
- Admin, Pharmacist, Store/Inventory and Management Viewer roles
- CSRF protection, prepared SQL, session authentication and audit logging foundation
- Localhost/LAN deployment and database backup support
